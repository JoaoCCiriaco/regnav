Navigate regulatory complexity. Understand risk. Plan compliance actions.

RegNav is a web-based regulatory risk intelligence platform designed to help organizations identify potential regulatory exposure, explore relevant regulatory frameworks, and organize preliminary compliance actions.

Key Features

Regulatory Risk Assessment — Evaluate potential regulatory exposure based on organizational characteristics.

Risk Analysis — Explore risk scores, levels, and contributing factors.

Regulatory Map — Identify potentially relevant regulatory frameworks.

Action Plan — Review recommended actions and track progress.

Interactive Dashboard — Monitor assessment results and action status.

Reports — Review assessment information in a structured format.

Technology

Built using Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML, and CSS.

Project Status

Public prototype — testing and evaluation phase.

RegNav is under active development. Its assessment results are preliminary and may evolve as the platform is improved.

Important Disclaimer

RegNav provides preliminary regulatory risk assessments for informational and educational purposes only.

The platform does not provide legal advice, perform formal compliance audits, or guarantee compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Organizations should verify applicable requirements using official regulatory sources and consult qualified professionals when necessary.
